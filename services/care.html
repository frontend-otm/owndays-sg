FAQ

  • 1. What damages are covered by OWNDAYS Care+?

    Your OWNDAYS Care+ covers any damage to the frame and/or lens, e.g. broken frame, scratched or cracked lens etc.
    You may choose to purchase OWNDAYS Care+ for your frame, or lens, or both. The coverage depends on which item OWNDAYS Care+ was purchased for.

  • 2. How much is OWNDAYS Care+?

    Enjoy the benefits of OWNDAYS Care+ at just 10% of the original retail price of your frame and/or lens. You may choose to purchase OWNDAYS Care+ for your frame, or lens, or both.

  • 3. What’s the difference between OWNDAYS Care+ and the standard OWNDAYS warranty?

    While the standard warranty covers damages to your frame due to manufacturing defects, OWNDAYS Care+ covers any damage to your frame and/or lens within 1 year of purchase.
    Items purchased at a discount can also covered by OWNDAYS Care+, unlike the standard warranty which is not applicable to sale items. Do note that the purchase of OWNDAYS Care+ is 10% of the original retail price.

  • 4. Where can I redeem my free replacement / 20% off next frame purchase?

    You can enjoy your OWNDAYS Care+ benefits at any OWNDAYS stores in Singapore.

  • 5. Can I purchase OWNDAYS Care+ with other discounts on my spectacles?

    Yes, you can. However, do note that the purchase of OWNDAYS Care+ is 10% of the original retail price of the frame + lens.

T&Cs

OWNDAYS Care+ is available for purchase for frame and/or lens. A 10% charge will be applicable on the original retail price of the selected item, i.e. if customer decides to purchase OWNDAYS Care+ for a lens, the 10% charge will be on the original retail price of the lens only, and only the lens is under the OWNDAYS Care+ warranty.
OWNDAYS Care+ is available for purchase with sunglasses, OWNDAYS PC glasses and all spectacle frames, excluding frames from collaboration collections and Limited Edition collection.
OWNDAYS Care+ expires one year from the date of collection. There is strictly no extension of expiry date and/or renewal of OWNDAYS Care+. 10% of the original retail price indicated on the product, rounded down to the nearest dollar, must be paid at the time of purchase. There is strictly no refund upon purchase of OWNDAYS Care+. OWNDAYS Care+ is non-transferrable and is valid for eyewear used by the same wearer only.
OWNDAYS Care+ covers damages to the frame and/or lens. Damaged frame and/or lens must be returned to OWNDAYS and will be replaced with a new frame and/or lens of the same model. In the event the same model is no longer in stock, a replacement product of the same or lower price will be provided. There will be no refund of the price difference if the replacement product is of a lower price. Replacement of damaged frame and/or lens is accepted at OWNDAYS retail stores in Singapore only.
For customers who have purchased OWNDAYS Care+ for frame and lens, they are entitled to a 1-time frame replacement and a 1-time lens replacement. The frame and lens replacement can be redeemed on separate occasions within the OWNDAYS Care+ validity period.
20%-off-new-purchase is valid for the purchase of spectacle frames with standard high index aspheric lenses only and excludes collaboration merchandise, limited-edition merchandise, sale items, sunglasses, OWNDAYS PC glasses, other ready-to-wear merchandise, accessories, Transitions, ZEISS and 1.74 index lens. The discount can only be redeemed unlimited number of times within the OWNDAYS Care+ validity period. The discount cannot be used in conjunction with other promotions and offers.
OWNDAYS Care+ is valid in Singapore only. Please retain the receipt as proof of purchase. The management reserves the right to amend the terms & conditions without prior notice.
OWNDAYS Care+ is only available for redemption at our retail stores.
