Is it true that UV rays entering your
eyes can cause suntan?

When UV rays enter the eyes, the brain sends a signal to the body to produce melanin which causes the skin to turn dark. Your sunscreen products will therefore be less effective if your eyes are left without UV protection. Don’t forget to safeguard your eyes against UV rays too!
